{# admin/_reason_modal.html -------------------------- ONE dialog for every action that needs a reason -- suspend, activate, archive, restore -- on both the Users and the Datasets pages. The button that opens it says what it is for, in data- attributes: data-bs-toggle="modal" data-bs-target="#reasonModal" data-action="" data-title="Archive account" data-subject="juan@example.com" data-note="They won't be able to sign in." data-verb="Archive" data-tone="danger" static/js/admin.js copies those into the dialog as it opens. One dialog rather than one per row: a table of 200 accounts would otherwise carry 800 hidden forms. Why a dialog and not confirm()/prompt(): the reason is the WHY in the audit trail, and a browser prompt gives it a one-line box, no hint of what is expected, and no way to say "that is too short" before the page reloads. The server checks the length again regardless -- the minlength here is a courtesy, not the guard. #}
