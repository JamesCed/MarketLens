How this plan is worked out
  • Diversity score uses the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI), the standard measure of market concentration: HHI is the sum of each industry's squared share of a barangay's businesses, rescaled so 0 means one industry holds everything and 100 means every industry has an equal share.
  • Saturation comes from the system's trained market model for each industry in each barangay, from the business counts on file (Google Places and LGU permit records, reconciled).
  • Regulate = saturated (≥ {{ t.saturated_at|int }}%) in at least {{ t.regulate_share_percent }}% of the barangays that have the industry, or an average saturation of {{ t.regulate_avg|int }}% or more. Encourage = an average saturation at least {{ t.encourage_margin|int }} points below the city's median industry ({{ plan.city_median_saturation if plan.city_median_saturation is not none else '—' }}%) with fewer than {{ t.encourage_max_saturated_percent }}% of its barangays saturated. Otherwise Monitor.
  • A barangay's diversity is scored only once counts exist for at least {{ t.min_industries }} of its industries, and measures how evenly its businesses spread across the industries on file.
  • Priority barangays have low diversity (below {{ t.low_diversity|int }}), one industry holding at least {{ t.dominance_percent }}% of businesses, or fewer than half the city's median businesses per 1,000 residents ({{ plan.median_access if plan.median_access is not none else '—' }}). The need score adds up how far past each line they are.
