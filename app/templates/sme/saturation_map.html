About this map's data
  • Measured: business counts looked up on Google Places or uploaded by the LGU (DTI permits), and population from the 2024 PSA census.
  • Estimated: any other count is filled in from nearby data so every barangay can still be scored. Estimated industry counts are marked (est.) in the barangay details.
  • Why estimates: Tarlac City has no live business registry, and checking all 20 industries in all 76 barangays would take thousands of paid Google calls. An estimate is replaced once the real count is fetched.
  • Boundaries: each barangay is drawn with its official boundary from the PSA / NAMRIA administrative boundary map (2023). The bold blue line is the city border.
  • How to use it: as a guide to where to look, not a survey.
