Click a barangay to isolate it (map, search, or the list on the right); double-click it again -- or click "Show All" -- to bring back every barangay.

The bold blue outline is Tarlac City's REAL administrative border. Each shaded zone inside it is the area closer to that barangay's own real, surveyed coordinate than to any other barangay's -- there's no publicly available per-barangay BOUNDARY dataset for Tarlac City (only point coordinates -- see app/services/choropleth_service.py), so a cell's exact INTERNAL edge (between two barangays) is a computed approximation rather than a traced administrative line, while its OUTER edge always stops exactly at the real city border. Precise per-barangay figures are always shown in the detail panel and location list. Household income has no publicly available per-barangay figure in the Philippines (see Reference/DATASETS.md), so it isn't shown as an overlay here -- population density is the real sociodemographic figure this map can back with sourced data.
