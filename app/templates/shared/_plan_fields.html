{# shared/_plan_fields.html -------------------------- The broader business parameters, as macros, used by all three places a plan is entered: the sign-up wizard (auth/register.html), the Home page's Add New Plan dialog, and the Home page's per-plan Edit dialog (which used to be a form in Settings > Business Preferences). One copy of the markup, driven by one script (static/js/plan_form.js), so the forms cannot drift apart -- which is exactly how "monthly revenue" had survived in one form after being dropped from another. plan_core_fields() is the WHOLE plan form body -- name, industry, sub-category, offering, location, stage, capital, employees, idea, price list -- and is what Add and Edit both render, so the two dialogs are the same form by construction rather than by care. The sign-up wizard keeps its own layout (input-group icons, per-step `required` applied by its script) but uses the same per-field macros below.
