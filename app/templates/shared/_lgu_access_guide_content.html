MarketLens has two LGU roles, held by two different people, and a System Admin who approves every LGU account before its role opens. Registering as LGU staff gives you the city-wide planning pages straight away; your LGU role follows once your request is approved.

{{ lgu_roles['encoder'] }}
Staff who prepare the data. Uploads zoning figures and permit lists to the staging area on Gov’t Data Upload, and can edit or archive an upload until it is approved.
{{ lgu_roles['steward'] }}
A supervisor or the office’s data officer. On Gov’t Data Review approves uploads, asks the encoder to fix or clean them, or rejects them.

The System Admin ({{ verification_office }}) approves LGU accounts and publishes approved data to the live dataset. Separate people for each step means no one can change the city’s figures alone.

Step by step

1
Register as LGU staff
Open the LGU registration form and fill in its three parts: account details (your name, official work e-mail{% if lgu_domains %} — {% for d in lgu_domains %}@{{ d }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}{% endif %} — and a password), your Tarlac City LGU context (city-wide or one barangay, your department or office, your job title), and the role you need with a short reason. Confirm the 6-digit code sent to your e-mail.
2
Your request is pending
After you sign in your request shows as Pending… and stays pending until the System Admin decides — please allow up to {{ review_hours }} hours. Meanwhile you can use the LGU Dashboard, the Diversification Plan, the Saturation Map and the Trend Reports. Gov’t Data Upload / Review shows as “Pending Request Access…” until then.
3
The System Admin decides
{{ verification_office }} checks your details with your office and approves your request with the right role, or rejects it with a reason. Either way you are told in MarketLens and by e-mail. An approval e-mail links straight to your page: Gov’t Data Upload for an Encoder, Gov’t Data Review for a Data Steward. If it was rejected, fix what the reason says and send a new request from your LGU Access Request page.
4
Keeping access current
Every {{ review_days }} days the System Admin re-checks each LGU account. If you move to another office or leave the LGU, your access is removed the same day.

City-wide or barangay scope

Choose Specific barangay if you work for one barangay (for example a Barangay Secretary). A barangay-scoped Encoder can only upload rows for that barangay, and a barangay-scoped Data Steward only reviews uploads for it. City and department staff choose City-wide.

Stay safe

  • Use your official work e-mail and a strong password that you use nowhere else.
  • MarketLens staff never ask for your password, your verification code or a government ID.
  • Do not share your account. Pretending to be LGU staff can be reported under the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (RA 10175).

What is kept about your request

Your office, job title, jurisdiction and role stay on your account. The request itself (with your reason) is seen only by the System Admin and deleted with the audit trail after it is decided. See the Privacy Policy. {% if verification_email %}Questions: {{ verification_email }}.{% endif %}
