MarketLens has two LGU roles, held by two different people, and a System Admin who verifies everyone before any LGU page opens. Choosing “LGU Staff / Official” at sign-up grants nothing by itself: anyone can pick it, so your head of office has to vouch for you first.

{{ lgu_roles['encoder'] }}
Staff who prepare the data (for example BPLO or zoning staff). Uploads zoning figures and permit lists to the staging area. Nothing goes live from an upload alone.
{{ lgu_roles['steward'] }}
A supervisor or the office’s data officer. Reviews each upload against the live figures and approves it or sends it back with a reason. Never reviews their own upload.

The System Admin ({{ verification_office }}) verifies people, then publishes approved data to the live dataset. Separate people for each step means no one can change the city’s figures alone, and every change has a second pair of eyes.

  • The name and official e-mail address of your head of office, who will endorse you.
  • Optional: a Discord account with two-factor authentication on, to join the MarketLens community.

Step by step

1
Join the MarketLens Discord (optional, recommended)
Open the {% if discord_url %}MarketLens Discord{% else %}MarketLens Discord{% endif %}, read #start-here and #lgu-access-guide, and in the onboarding questions choose “LGU staff – applicant”. That role only opens the help channels: it is not verification and gives no access to MarketLens.
2
Create your MarketLens account
Sign up and choose LGU Staff / Official. Under “Your LGU office” give your office, position, the role you need, and your head of office’s name and official e-mail (plus your Discord username if you joined). You get a reference code such as LGU-7K3QPM. Your account works, but LGU pages stay closed until you are verified.
3
Your head of office endorses you
They e-mail {{ verification_email or 'the System Admin' }} from their official address, quoting your reference code (template below). On Discord you may open a private thread in #lgu-verification with only your code and name.
4
The System Admin verifies you
{{ verification_office }} checks that the endorsement came from the official address and calls your office on its number in the LGU directory (never a number sent in chat). Then they approve your request in MarketLens with the right role, or decline it with a reason you can fix.
5
You are in
You are notified in MarketLens and by e-mail, and your LGU pages open. On Discord the admin then gives you the LGU Encoder or LGU Data Steward role, which opens the private LGU channels. Discord roles always follow MarketLens, never the other way round.
6
Keeping access current
Every {{ review_days }} days the System Admin re-checks each LGU account with its office. If you move to another office or leave the LGU, your head of office tells the System Admin and your access is removed the same day, in MarketLens and on Discord.

Stay safe

Do
  • Turn on two-factor authentication for Discord and use a strong, unique MarketLens password.
  • Talk about your request only in your private thread, and only with members who have the MarketLens Admin role.
  • Report anyone asking for passwords, codes or IDs, or pretending to be an admin.
Never
  • Post or send your password, a verification code, a government ID, a payslip or your personal number on Discord. MarketLens never asks for them.
  • Share your MarketLens account or sign in for someone else.
  • Ask for a role you are not endorsed for. Pretending to be LGU staff can be reported under the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (RA 10175).

What is kept about your request

Your office, position and role stay on your account. The verification details (your head of office’s name and e-mail, your Discord username) are stored encrypted, seen only by the System Admin, and deleted with the audit trail after the request is decided; a request not verified within 60 days expires. See the Privacy Policy.
