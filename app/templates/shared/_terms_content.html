1. What MarketLens is

MarketLens is a decision support system that estimates how crowded (saturated) each industry is in Tarlac City’s 76 barangays and helps business owners and the city plan where businesses should open. It is an undergraduate capstone project. By creating an account or using MarketLens you agree to these Terms and to the Privacy Policy.

3. Disclaimer of warranty

The market saturation scores (MSI), competitor density ratings, business viability scores, quarterly outlooks, location recommendations and AI-written explanations are analytical estimates produced by statistical and machine-learning models from the data available to the system. They are provided for decision support only.

  • They are not financial, investment, legal, tax or business advice, and they are not a promise that a business will succeed or fail.
  • They can be incomplete, out of date or wrong: competitor counts depend on public listings and uploaded records, some figures are estimates (the pages label them), and the models were trained partly on synthetic data.
  • MarketLens is provided “as is” and “as available”, without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including accuracy, fitness for a particular purpose and uninterrupted service.
  • Before you commit money, verify the figures yourself and consult the proper offices (for example the DTI, the Business Permits and Licensing Office) and qualified professionals.
