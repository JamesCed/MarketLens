1. What MarketLens is

MarketLens is a decision support system that estimates how crowded (saturated) each industry is in Tarlac City’s 76 barangays and helps business owners and the city plan where businesses should open. It is an undergraduate capstone project. Its forecasts, scores and AI-written explanations are decision support, not financial, legal or investment advice. Some figures are estimates; the pages label them as such.

2. Your account

  • Give true, complete and current information, and keep it up to date in Settings.
  • Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • Use the system only for lawful business planning. Do not try to access other accounts, other roles’ pages, or the system’s data in ways the pages do not offer.
  • An administrator may suspend or archive an account that breaks these terms. Archived accounts can be restored.

3. Data Privacy Notice

MarketLens processes personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect. Your name, email address, gender, birthday, contact number and address; the business plans you enter (business name, industry, barangay, capital, staff, prices and ideas); and a record of actions taken in the system (who did what, when, from which page and device) for security.

Why. To create and secure your account, to produce your forecasts and recommendations, to let the city plan with aggregated, city-wide figures, to send the alerts you switch on, and to keep an audit trail.

Who can see it. Your plans and forecasts are visible to you. LGU officials see city-wide totals and trends, not your personal details. Administrators manage accounts and see your name and email; your other personal details are shown to them masked. Your details are never sold. AI-written explanations are generated from your plan’s figures, not from your personal details.

How it is protected. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash. Your gender, birthday, contact number and address are encrypted in the database. Pages are served over HTTPS, every form is protected against forged requests, and each role can open only its own pages.

How long it is kept. For as long as your account exists. Records are archived rather than deleted so they cannot disappear by accident; a plan moved to Trash is deleted for good after 30 days.

Your rights. You may ask to see, correct, or have your personal information archived, and you may object to its processing, by contacting {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL or 'the system administrator' }}. You can correct most details yourself in Settings.
