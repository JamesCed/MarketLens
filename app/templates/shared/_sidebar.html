Where the numbers come from

Competitor counts are real businesses from the Google Places API, stored in this system. Saturation and viability are produced by a trained Random Forest model; population figures are the 2024 PSA census. Anything the system has not measured yet is labelled (estimated) on screen rather than presented as fact.
