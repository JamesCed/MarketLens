{# shared/_contact_modal.html ----------------------------- The Contact Us dialog, included once from base.html so every dashboard has it -- SME, LGU and Admin alike. WHY A DIALOG RATHER THAN A BARE mailto: LINK. The footer entry used to be the plain text "Contact Us" with nothing behind it. Replacing it with a mailto: would technically work and would still leave the two questions people actually have unanswered: who is on the other end, and what should I put in the message. A support inbox that receives "it's not working" from an address it cannot place costs more time than it saves. So the dialog says who to write to, what each kind of enquiry needs, and prefills a subject and body for the common cases. The mailto: link is still there -- it is the button inside. It lives in base.html rather than in each dashboard because the same dialog is reached from the SME footer, the LGU dashboard footer and the Admin dashboard footer, and three copies would drift. SUPPORT_EMAIL comes from app/config.py through the context processor in app/__init__.py, so the address is written down once. #}

What to write to us about
A figure looks wrong
A competitor count, population or saturation score that does not match what you know on the ground. Tell us the barangay and the industry.
Access problems
You cannot sign in, your verification code never arrived, or your account has the wrong role.
LGU data uploads
Your dataset was refused, or the figures did not change after uploading it. Attach the file if you can — the error message names the row and column.
Anything else
Questions about how the recommendations are produced, or about the data sources behind them.
