What it does

Four questions, answered from real data

Every figure comes from competitor counts collected for Tarlac City, the 2024 PSA population of each barangay, and a Random Forest trained on them — not from a survey and not from a guess.

How crowded is it?

A saturation index for your industry in your barangay, with the viability score and the confidence the model actually produced.

Where exactly?

A choropleth of all 76 barangays, clipped to the city's real boundary and shaded from high opportunity through to saturated.

Where else could I go?

Locations you could realistically enter, ranked, each with an AI-estimated ROI timeframe and the reasoning behind it.

Which way is it moving?

Monthly and quarterly trends from 2020 to today, including the pandemic contraction and the recovery after it.

{# ------------------------------ how ------------------------------ #}
How it works

From a business idea to a decision you can defend

  1. 1

    Describe the plan

    Industry, target barangay, starting capital, stage. That is the whole input.

  2. 2

    The engine scores it

    Competitor density, demand and socio-demographic weight combine into a market saturation index, and K-Means places the result in one of four tiers.

  3. 3

    Act on the reasoning

    You get the score, the map, alternative locations and a plain-language account of what drove it — not a number on its own.
