Community guidelines

  • Be respectful. No insults or bad words.
  • No scams or selling schemes. No investment offers or recruiting.
  • No personal info. Don't share phone numbers or addresses, yours or anyone's.
  • Stay on business topics. Questions, tips and local news are all welcome.

Posts are checked before they appear, so yours may take a little while to show up.
